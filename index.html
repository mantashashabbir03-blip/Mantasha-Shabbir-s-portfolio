AAGAMAN — ML-Driven Dynamic ETA Prediction

Smart India Hackathon 2026 · Team MindZed
Theme: Smart Automation Software Problem Statement: SIH26028

Proposed an AI- and machine-learning-driven system to dynamically predict Expected Time of Arrival (ETA) for coaching trains by recalculating delay changes across each segment of a train's route as new data arrives.

  • Three-tier prediction approach: LightGBM for segment-delay prediction, GRU neural networks for delay momentum and recovery, and graph propagation to model delays across shared tracks.
  • Real-world conditions: Designed to account for congestion, fog, maintenance, and cascading delays, while providing ETA confidence intervals.
  • Resilient predictions: Proposed a five-level fallback approach to keep ETA estimates available during GPS data gaps.
  • Proposed technology stack: Python, TypeScript, LightGBM, PyTorch, FastAPI, Next.js/React, PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB, Redis Streams or Apache Kafka, and Docker/Kubernetes.

Focus: Explainable predictions, passenger information, railway operations, and more reliable arrival estimates.
